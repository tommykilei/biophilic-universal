Maasai Mara

Maasai Mara National Reserve in southwestern Kenya is famous for its open plains, big cats and the great wildebeest migration, which usually peaks in the second half of the year.

  • Big cats
  • Wildebeest migration
  • Open plains
Enquire about Maasai Mara

Amboseli

Amboseli National Park is known for its elephants and for wide views of Mount Kilimanjaro rising across the border in Tanzania.

  • Elephants
  • Views of Kilimanjaro
Enquire about Amboseli

Tsavo

Tsavo East and Tsavo West are among Kenya's largest national parks, with red earth, wide landscapes and plenty of space to explore.

  • Vast parks
  • Red earth
  • Elephants
Enquire about Tsavo

Lake Nakuru

Lake Nakuru National Park surrounds a Rift Valley lake and is known for its birdlife and its rhinos.

  • Birdlife
  • Rhinos
  • Rift Valley lake
Enquire about Lake Nakuru

Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is Africa's second-highest peak. Trekkers come for cool air, changing scenery and big views.

  • Trekking
  • Mountain scenery
Enquire about Mount Kenya

Diani Beach

Diani Beach on Kenya's south coast offers white sand and warm Indian Ocean water. It suits a slow holiday, or a rest after a safari.

  • White sand
  • Indian Ocean
  • South coast
Enquire about Diani Beach

Lake Naivasha

Lake Naivasha is a freshwater lake in the Rift Valley, popular for its birdlife and relaxed lakeside setting.

  • Freshwater lake
  • Birdlife
  • Rift Valley
Enquire about Lake Naivasha

Samburu

Samburu National Reserve in northern Kenya has a drier landscape and wildlife you will not see everywhere, such as Grevy's zebra and reticulated giraffe.

  • Dry-country wildlife
  • Grevy's zebra
  • Reticulated giraffe
Enquire about Samburu

Who we are

Biophilic Universal Tours & Travels Ltd is a Kenyan tourism company based in Nairobi. We organise tours and safaris across Kenya and provide car hire, and we can help you find an Airbnb stay to go with your trip. We welcome both local and international travellers.

The word biophilic describes a love of the natural world. It suits our work: Kenya's wildlife, mountains, lakes and coast are at the heart of the trips we plan.
